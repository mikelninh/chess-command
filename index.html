UNOFFICIAL FAN IMPLEMENTATION

The television prop came first. The playable rules came later.

This build follows the Federation Revised Standard 5.0 family of fan rules. It is a deterministic game-engine experiment, not an official Star Trek product.

01

64 squares, unusual geometry

Three fixed 4×4 main boards plus four movable 2×2 attack boards.

02

Projected movement

Rooks, bishops, queens, kings and pawns keep recognisable chess geometry while traversing elevations.

03

Movable terrain

Moving an eligible attack board consumes a turn. A single owned pawn may ride with it.

04

King safety

Legal moves and attack-board actions are rejected when they leave your king exposed.

05

Chess rules retained

Check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, promotion and standard en passant are modelled in the beta engine.

06

Why “beta”?

The common rule path is playable and tested; obscure Tri-D-specific edge interactions still deserve more conformance tests.
